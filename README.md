### NYC-collisions-analysis

## Introduction:
Road traffic collisions are a major public safety concern. This project analyzes NYC motor vehicle collision data using Microsoft Excel to identify collision patterns, contributing factors, and areas requiring improved road safety.

## Key Metrics:
- Total recorded collisions: 238,421
- Persons reported injured: 116,226
- Persons reported killed: 635
- Highest monthly collision share: March (10.5%)
- Highest weekday collision count: Friday (37,496)
- Peak collision hour: 4:00 p.m. (15,065)
- Borough with the highest collision count: Brooklyn (76,416)

## Analysis:
- Monthly Collision Trends
  
March recorded the highest share of collisions, while December recorded the lowest. This highlights monthly variations in collision frequency.
- Day and Time Patterns
  
Friday had the highest number of collisions, while 4:00 p.m. was the peak collision hour with a smaller peak at 8AM, which matches commuting times. These patterns can help identify periods for further road-safety investigation.
- Geographical Distribution of collisions by Street and Borough
  
Brooklyn(Borough) recorded the highest number of collisions, accounting for approximately 32% of the total collisions while Belt Parkway Street had the most collisions which represents 1.56% of all reported accidents.
- Contributing Factors
  
Driver inattention/distraction was the most frequently reported contributing factor for all accidents with 58,308 records while for fatal accidents, the most common contributing factor was unspecified(175) asides which unsafe speed was another most contributing factor(130).
- Vehicle Type
  
Passenger vehicles accounted for approximately 84.6% of the records, making them the most represented vehicle category.

## Conclusion and Recommendations:
- Run road safety campaigns and extra patrols from January to May.
- Increase enforcement from 3-6PM especially on Fridays.
- Add speed cameras and lane management on Belt Parkway and other high collision street.
- Improve recording of contributing factors in fatal cases.
- More traffic officers and safety improvements(signals, crossings) in Brooklyn.
